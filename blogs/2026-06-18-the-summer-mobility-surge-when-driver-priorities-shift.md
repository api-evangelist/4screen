What happens when millions of people leave their routines behind at the same time? Every summer, travel patterns shift dramatically. Commuters become holidaymakers, familiar routes give way to new destinations, and drivers spend more time navigating unfamiliar locations, searching for places to stop, and discovering destinations they may never have visited before.
